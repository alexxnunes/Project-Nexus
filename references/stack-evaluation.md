# Stack evaluation

Compare options using weighted or qualitative criteria:

1. adequacy to the problem and requirements;
2. experience and maintenance capacity of the team;
3. maturity, documentation and community;
4. stability, compatibility and version cycle;
5. required performance;
6. safety and maintenance history;
7. ecosystem and integrations;
8. cost of hosting, licenses and operation;
9. ease of testing, deployment, backup and observability;
10. risk of lock-in and ease of migration.

For each technology, record: role, version or range, reason, discarded alternatives, restrictions and update policy.

Check current versions in reliable sources when the decision depends on recent information. Don't invent benchmarks, costs or compatibility. Avoid adding technology without concrete benefit.
