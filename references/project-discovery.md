# Discovery and planning

## Discovery Interview

Investigate in stages, without dumping an entire questionnaire:
1. What problem will be solved and for whom?
2. What is the minimum useful result?
3. Which flows are essential?
4. What is explicitly not part of the scope?
5. Are there existing systems, data or processes?
6. What budget, deadline, infrastructure, licensing and privacy restrictions exist?
7. Who operates, maintains and supports?
8. How will success be measured?

Ask only what is necessary to move forward. Separate blocking questions from those that can be left for later.

## Planning deliverables

- vision and objectives;
- personas/actors and flows;
- MVP scope and out of scope;
- functional and non-functional requirements;
- candidate architecture;
- recommended stack and alternatives;
- integrations and dependencies;
- risks and questions;
- delivery phases;
- acceptance criteria;
- test and operation plan.

## Plan quality

The plan must be executable, but not feign precision. Do not estimate dates or effort without assumptions. Every phase must have verifiable deliverables and explicit dependencies.
