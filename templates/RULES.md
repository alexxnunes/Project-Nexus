# Project rules

## Mandatory rules
- Respect approved architecture and decisions.
- Do not introduce stack, service or structural dependency without approval.
- Do not store secrets in the repository or documentation.
- Do not change contracts or data in an incompatible way without an approved plan.
- Do not declare tests performed without evidence.
- Update documentation when relevant changes are approved.
